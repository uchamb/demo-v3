# demo3 — modeling notes

This interactive coastal scene contains 50 selectable structures: 6 towers, 34 garden residences, 9 pavilions, and a terraced seafront building. All dimensions, floor counts, positions, and landscaping details are illustrative.

Geometry is built procedurally with Three.js. No external services or project imagery are required at runtime. Library and font license notices are in `public/licenses/`.
